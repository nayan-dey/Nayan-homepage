# Icon notice

Two small icon sets appear in the interface. Company and project logos remain original brand assets (see public/brands/SOURCES.md).

Social glyphs (GitHub, LinkedIn, X, Instagram) in src/SocialIcons.tsx come from [Simple Icons v11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons), distributed under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/LICENSE.md). Original paths are retained.

Product tiles in src/Icons.tsx (Griffin, CLI, MCP, Desktop) are 16px rounded squares; the MCP glyph is the Model Context Protocol mark from Simple Icons (CC0). Agent marks under public/agents and the Safeguard monogram and app icon under public/brands are original brand assets, see public/brands/SOURCES.md.

Outline icons in src/Icons.tsx were drawn for this site: platform marks for the Safeguard chips (Web, Desktop, iOS, Android, CLI, MCP, Docs), a code mark for chips that link to public source, and the sun and moon in the theme toggle. One family, 16-unit grid, 1.5px stroke, currentColor. The iOS and Android marks are generic device and robot-head outlines, not the Apple or Android trademarks.

Every icon link or button has an accessible name; SVGs are hidden from assistive technology to avoid repeated labels. Brand trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
