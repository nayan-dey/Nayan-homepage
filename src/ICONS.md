# Social icon notice

The rendered interface uses icons only for GitHub, LinkedIn, X and Instagram social links. Company and project logos remain original brand assets.

The four social glyphs in src/SocialIcons.tsx come from [Simple Icons v11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons), distributed under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/LICENSE.md). Original paths are retained; decorative titles are removed because the links already have visible names. SVGs use currentColor and are hidden from assistive technology to avoid repeated labels.

Brand trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
