## Self-critique against the generic default

A tinted near-black canvas and a warm cream canvas are both listed AI defaults, and the brief pins both: Linear's quiet neutral gray for dark, and "minimal pastel" for light. Within those constraints: the dark canvas is a neutral `#101113` rather than a purple or blue-black; the light canvas is warm champagne with cream raised surfaces and a subdued mauve accent, following revision 31's request for more warmth; there is no accent colour at rest in either theme; depth comes from a one-pixel inset highlight and a two-pixel shadow rather than gradients or glows; and the only blur at rest is the sticky bar. The navigation is text on the bar's own surface with a sliding underline, so it reads as the header rather than a control dropped into it; revision 27's segmented strip was rejected for exactly that reason. Rejected: a bottom tab bar (the visitor asked for the links in the header), a replacement headline on Home, per-paragraph staggers on every view change (only the first paint staggers), a separate System option in the toggle (the page follows the OS initially, then remembers a manual choice), and any "trending skills" panel (the trends only informed which proven work leads). Nothing is in all caps, nothing is numbered, no arrows on links, no em dashes.

## Components and behaviour

- Top bar: sticky, translucent canvas with 12px backdrop blur. Phones: the vector wordmark and the theme toggle on a 44px row, then the four navigation links on a 44px row, 90px in all. From 700px: one 56px row with the wordmark left and the links and toggle right. A hairline fades in after 48px of scroll on browsers with scroll-driven animations and is always shown elsewhere.
- Navigation: Home, Work, About, Contact as `<a role="tab">` inside the `<nav role="tablist">`, each a 44px tall label (56px on desktop) with 10px side padding and no box of its own; the first label's text aligns with the name. `aria-selected`, `aria-controls` and roving `tabindex` are live; Arrow keys, Home and End move focus and switch instantly; Enter and Space activate instantly. The selected label is ink, the rest muted. One 2px underline, as wide as the label's text, sits on the bar's bottom edge and moves with `translate` and `width` transitions (260ms, strong ease-out), so it slides between links and retargets if tapped again mid-move; it is re-measured on resize and after fonts load. Before the script measures it, the selected link draws its own underline with an inset shadow, so prerendered HTML shows the right state. Keyboard focus is a 2px accent ring inset 6px inside the label.
- Views: four prerendered `role="tabpanel"` sections. Home, Work, About and Contact map to `/`, `#work`, `#about` and `#contact`; taps call `history.pushState`, `popstate` switches back, and a 10-line script in `index.html` sets `html[data-view]` from the hash before paint so deep links never flash Home. CSS shows only the matching panel (`display: none` for the rest, so hidden content is neither focusable nor read). Without JavaScript the attribute is absent and all four views render in order.
- View transition: a tap marks the current panel as leaving (opacity 0, 16px slide, 3px blur over 110ms as a CSS transition) and after 110ms the next panel is shown and enters through one Web Animations call (opacity, 24px slide from the opposite side, 4px blur over 220ms). The direction follows the tab order relative to the panel on screen: moving to a later tab exits left and enters from the right, moving to an earlier tab exits right and enters from the left. Keyboard changes skip both. Choosing another tab mid-transition retargets the exit, including its direction, without restarting or locking input; choosing the leaving tab again simply lets it slide back. The page scrolls to the top instantly when it was scrolled. `prefers-reduced-motion` makes every change instant.
- Theme toggle: a 44px button with a 32px raised face holding the sun and moon, both always in the DOM and cross-faded exactly as better-ui prescribes (opacity 0 to 1, scale 0.25 to 1, blur 4px to 0 over 300ms with `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`). The visible icon is the current theme. First paint follows a saved choice, else the OS, via the same early script, which also sets `theme-color`. Toggling injects a one-frame `transition: none` style (the icons are exempted), flips `html[data-theme]`, forces a reflow and removes the override, so colours snap rather than smear. Every manual choice is saved to localStorage and kept across reloads and OS changes; until a choice is made the page follows OS changes live. Storage errors are swallowed and the choice lasts for the page.
- Home: "Founding engineer at Safeguard" as the `h1` (17px, 19px on desktop, with Safeguard linked), two short paragraphs, then Email me, See work (switches to the Work view), GitHub and X. On phones the icons take their own line, flush with the text. LinkedIn and Instagram were removed from Home and Contact at Nayan's request. No headline and no Now row.
- Work: three hairline-separated rows, each with the original company mark at 28px, company link, role, period on the right, one intro paragraph, a list of short first-person lines (14px, 15px on desktop, a 4px muted dot, no boxes) and a row of wrapping link chips. Safeguard has Web, Desktop, iOS, Android, CLI, MCP and Docs, each with a 14px outline icon from `src/Icons.tsx` and a full accessible name (for example "Safeguard for iOS on the App Store"), linking to app.safeguard.sh, safeguard.sh/download, the App Store, Google Play, mcp.safeguard.sh and docs.safeguard.sh. HSX shows TBN247, Vegio and Jayate Farms with 16px original marks; Erex shows Food Comet and a QR Cuisine chip with the code icon linking to the public repository. The Play Store screenshots remain in `public/projects` but are not shown.
- About: three short first-person paragraphs, then Skills under a hairline: a definition list of four groups (Agents, Backends, Product, Security) with static chips based on the contribution histories. On wide screens the group label sits in a 120px column beside the chips.
- Contact: one address row, no field or card: the full address as a 15px ink mailto link with a 44px hit area, then the standard 32px secondary Copy button 14px to its right with its three labels stacked for a stable width; then GitHub and X under "Elsewhere". LinkedIn and Instagram were removed from this view at Nayan's request. Copy uses the Clipboard API, then a selected-textarea fallback that restores focus. States: Copy, Copying (disabled), Copied (accent text, polite live region, resets after 2.4s) or a visible message directing the visitor to select the address. There is deliberately no second Email me button.
- Footer: name and the source link.

Buttons are 32px (28px small) with a 7px radius; the primary is ink on canvas with an inset top highlight and a 1px shadow, the secondary is a raised surface with a 1px border, inset highlight and the same shadow. Chips are 26px with a 6px radius and the same raised treatment when they are links; static skill chips are flat with a border. Pressing scales buttons, tabs, chips and the toggle to 0.96 over 150ms and flattens the shadow. Every button and link chip extends its hit area to 44px with a centered pseudo-element; wrapped chip rows keep 44px per row so hit areas never overlap. Social, theme and footer controls are 44px boxes. Focus is a 2px accent outline (inset on tabs). Safe-area insets pad the bar and page.

Motion summary: press 150ms, hover and colour 120ms, tab indicator 260ms, view leave 110ms (16px sideways) and enter 220ms (24px from the opposite side), icon cross-fade 300ms, first-paint arrival 480ms with a 50ms stagger across the hero's four blocks (CSS keyframes, so prerendered content still appears without JavaScript; `html[data-booted]` switches them off 900ms after load so later view changes never replay them). Easings: a strong ease-out `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` for anything entering or moving, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` for the icon swap. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes all animation and transitions, makes view and theme changes instant and keeps the bar hairline visible. Forced-colors mode draws real borders and outlines the selected tab.
